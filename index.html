Your Experience

Kremasti — your island base

Kremasti is a peaceful, authentic village offering the best of Rhodes without the tourist crowds. From here, the whole island is yours to discover.

Beach Direct Access
Step out of your apartment and onto Kremasti Beach in minutes. Ideal for morning swims and windsurfing.
Prime Connectivity
Minutes from Rhodes International Airport for effortless arrivals, and perfectly positioned for island day trips.
Pristine Old Town
A short journey brings you to Rhodes Old Town, a breathtaking UNESCO World Heritage site.
Events & Day Trips
Discover Lindos, Faliraki, and the Valley of the Butterflies, all easily accessible from your base.
Local Tavernas
Authentic Greek dining, supermarkets, and quaint cafes are all within a relaxed walking distance.
Personalized Care
Your host, Yannis, is always available to organize transfers and share his favorite hidden island gems.
